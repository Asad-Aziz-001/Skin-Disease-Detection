About The Project

What is DermaScan AI?

DermaScan AI is a Final Year Project by BS Computer Science students of Federal Urdu University of Arts, Science & Technology (FUUAST), Karachi. This project brings clinical-grade skin disease detection to everyone using cutting-edge deep learning technology.

🎯
Problem Statement
Lack of accessible dermatology services in remote areas. Need for quick, affordable preliminary skin disease screening.
💡
Our Solution
AI-powered web application that provides instant skin disease detection with 94% accuracy using deep learning.
🎓
Academic Context
Final Year Project supervised by faculty of Computer Science department, FUUAST.
🌍
Social Impact
Making dermatology accessible to underserved communities, reducing healthcare costs and wait times.
Google BiT Model
Transfer learning on ResNet architecture, pre-trained on 300M+ images for superior feature extraction.
31 Disease Classes
From Basal Cell Carcinoma to Leprosy — comprehensive coverage of dermatological conditions.
Explainable AI
Confidence scores for each prediction with top-5 accuracy metrics and visual charts.

Detection Library

31 Diseases We Detect

Our BiT model recognizes 31 skin conditions with high clinical accuracy — from carcinomas to infectious diseases.

⚠️
Basal Cell Carcinoma
Most common form of skin cancer — appears as a pearly bump, often on sun-exposed areas.
94% Accuracy
🦠
Herpes Simplex
Viral infection causing painful blisters on lips, mouth, or genital areas.
92% Accuracy
🟡
Impetigo
Highly contagious bacterial infection causing red sores, often in children.
95% Accuracy
🐛
Larva Migrans
Cutaneous condition caused by hookworm larvae migration under the skin.
91% Accuracy
🔬
Leprosy (3 Types)
Borderline, Lepromatous & Tuberculoid — chronic infectious disease affecting skin & nerves.
90% Accuracy
🧬
Darier's Disease
Genetic disorder causing greasy, warty bumps on skin — also known as Keratosis follicularis.
89% Accuracy
